About Me

The person behind the systems, schedules,
and support.

I'm Marneh John Luceñara, an Executive Assistant and Operations Professional with a background in education, research, communication, and choral music. My career has taken me through classrooms, research projects, choir rehearsals, and executive offices — and each chapter has shaped how I work today.

Where it started

A background in language, communication, and learning.

I graduated Cum Laude from Cebu Technological University – Argao Campus in 2016 with a degree in English, major in Applied Linguistics. My undergraduate thesis, Sensory Style Preferences in English Language Learning of HM and AB-English College Students, reflected my interest in language, communication, and the different ways people learn.

During my university years, I was also a member of Southern Ripples, our campus publication, where I later served as Features Editor during my fourth year. I was also a Tenor 1 member of the CTU-Argao Chorale.

Looking back, these experiences gave me an early foundation in writing, research, communication, collaboration, and attention to detail — skills I still rely on in my work today.

The educator

Returning to my alma mater as an instructor.

After graduating, I returned to Cebu Technological University – Argao Campus, where I served as a Part-Time English Instructor and Senior High School Research Instructor from July 2017 to May 2022, under the AB English and AB Literature Programs of the College of Arts and Sciences.

I taught courses including Speech Communication, Research, Literature, and Introduction to Linguistics. Beyond teaching, I also served as a Research Adviser, NSTP Coordinator, and Extension Program Training Expert.

My years in education taught me a great deal about preparation, organization, communication, and working with people with different needs and perspectives. They also strengthened my ability to take complex information, understand what matters most, and communicate it clearly.

My academic background and faculty affiliation can also be found through the official CTU-Argao faculty profile.

Choral leadership

Learning to lead from behind the music.

Music has been another important part of my life. I began my involvement with the CTU-Argao Chorale as a Tenor 1 during my university years. By my fourth year in 2016, I had started taking on a greater role in training the group, and when I officially began teaching in 2017, I continued serving as its Choirmaster and Conductor through 2022.

My work involved training singers for performances and competitions, arranging music, conducting rehearsals, and helping prepare the group to perform at its best. The experience taught me how to coordinate people with different personalities, strengths, and needs while keeping everyone focused on a shared goal.

The choir was a consistent placer in the PASUC choral competition and was named Grand Champion of Harana Alang Kang Maria in 2017. I was also involved with the Argao Minstrels, a youth community-based group.

On leading a choir
"Choral leadership taught me that good preparation often happens quietly in the background, long before anyone sees the final performance — a lesson I carry into executive support today."
The transition

From the classroom to the executive office.

In September 2022, I began transitioning into remote executive support. I wanted to build a career that allowed me to work remotely while continuing to use the skills I had developed through teaching, research, and leadership.

I started my EA career with Athena and later worked directly with executives and founders, including my role as Executive Assistant to the President at Meson Capital. Since then, my work has expanded into executive support, premium client support, and remote operations.

The transition felt natural in many ways. The organization and attention to detail I developed as an educator became useful in managing executive priorities. My communication skills helped me work effectively with different personalities and stakeholders. My experience as a choirmaster taught me to anticipate needs, coordinate people, stay calm when things change, and pay attention to the small details that affect the whole.

Today

Executive Assistant & Operations Professional.

Today, I work as an Executive Assistant and Operations Professional, supporting executives and teams through thoughtful coordination, organization, communication, and attention to detail.

What I enjoy most about this work is being able to work directly with visionaries and executives — helping bring structure to their priorities, keeping moving parts organized, and creating the operational clarity that allows them to focus on the work only they can do.

My background may span several different fields, but the thread connecting them has always been the same: I enjoy helping people and teams work better. Whether I was teaching a class, advising a research project, preparing a choir for a competition, or supporting an executive, I have always found meaning in making things more organized, more manageable, and easier for others to navigate.
